# Research Questions

These are initial questions and should be refined after the literature review and system survey.

## RQ1
Which free and open-source agentic AI systems currently support meaningful self-hosted or on-premise deployment?

## RQ2
How do selected systems differ in functional capability, deployment complexity, interoperability, and resource requirements?

## RQ3
How reliably do selected self-hosted agentic AI systems complete standardized tool-using tasks under comparable conditions?

## RQ4
What security risks and control mechanisms are relevant when self-hosted agents can access documents, databases, APIs, files, or executable tools?

## RQ5
What trade-offs exist between fully self-hosted deployment and cloud-dependent deployment with respect to capability, privacy, control, cost, and operational effort?

## RQ6
Which architectural patterns appear most suitable for enterprise-style use cases such as internal knowledge access, operational workflows, maintenance information, and controlled business-system actions?

## Notes on hypotheses
Do not invent hypotheses only because some research projects use them. If the final study is exploratory/comparative, research questions may be sufficient. Add hypotheses only where the team has a justified expectation that can be tested.
